## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide:

- **Contact form**: name, email, agency, service of interest, message
- **Checklist form**: name, email, agency, role
- **Waitlist form**: name, email, agency, role
- **Calendar booking**: name, email, and any context you share when scheduling
  a consultation through the Google Calendar embed

We **do not collect Protected Health Information (PHI)**. Do not submit
patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, or any other
identifiable patient information through any form on this site. We are not a
covered entity or business associate under HIPAA.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

- Respond to your inquiry or fulfill your request (e.g., send you the free
  checklist)
- Communicate with you about services you&rsquo;ve expressed interest in
- Send occasional updates about new content, services, or course launches
  (you can opt out at any time)
- Maintain site security and prevent abuse

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with unrelated
third parties.

## 3. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party services to operate. Each has its own
privacy practices:

- **GitHub Pages** (hosting): receives your IP address and request data
- **Google Forms** (form submissions): stores your form responses on
  Google&rsquo;s infrastructure
- **Google Workspace Calendar** (scheduling): collects booking details you
  provide

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these providers.

## 4. Cookies and Local Storage

The site itself does not set cookies or use local storage. Third-party
services listed in Section 3 (Google Forms, Google Workspace Calendar) may
set their own cookies when you interact with their embeds. You can control
cookies through your browser settings or by enabling Do Not Track.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain form submissions and email correspondence for as long as needed to
provide our services and comply with legal obligations. You can request
deletion of your data at any time (see Section 7).

## 6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide, including
HTTPS encryption in transit, access controls on our systems, and the use of
reputable third-party services with strong security postures. No method of
transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to minimize risk.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
- Request correction or deletion of your personal information
- Opt out of marketing communications
- Disable cookies through your browser settings

To exercise any of these rights, email us at
[info@tahsolutions.com](mailto:info@tahsolutions.com). We will respond within
30 days.

## 8. Children&rsquo;s Privacy

Our site and services are not directed to children under 13. We do not
knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child
has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted
on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

## 10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at
[info@tahsolutions.com](mailto:info@tahsolutions.com).
